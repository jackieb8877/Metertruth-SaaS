# Merchandising Map v1

Three job-led collections should anchor the 20–50 SKU store:

### 1. Mascotas en casa
- Kit Muda Cero — HERO — test €39.90 first, then €34.90.
- Reusable pet-hair roller — CROSS-SELL.
- Pet cleaning gloves — UPSELL.

### 2. Mascotas en movimiento

### 3. Viaje y coche
- Compression packing cubes — TEST — €39.90 / €49.90.

## Creative direction
Use real product footage as the proof layer. Prefer phone-shot UGC, macro tactile video, uncut demonstrations and real before/after sequences. Avoid generic AI-looking people or pets, fake social proof and decorative 3D. Use 3D only when it clarifies construction, fit or how a product works.

## Homepage order
1. Motion-led brand hero.
2. Three job tiles.
3. HERO products.
4. Short demonstration strip.
5. Collection rails.
6. Bundles and cross-sells.
7. Trust and FAQ.

## Store Builder handoff
- Build around the three job-led collections, not supplier categories.
- Real looping video in hero and collection headers.
- Compatibility/fit modules on Muda Cero, dog-cover and sunshade PDPs.
- Data-driven bundle and cross-sell slots.
- 3D remains secondary to real footage.

## SEO handoff
- Pet hair removal / dog grooming at home.
- Dog car protection / pet travel.
- Packing organization / windshield sun protection.
- FAQ around coat type, textile compatibility, vehicle measurements, waterproofing, wash care, compression and sizing.
- Do not index thin TEST pages before product data and useful copy are complete.

## Catalogue rule
Do not open a public collection until it has at least 3 credible SKUs or one HERO plus two coherent complements. Reject random products that do not extend one of the three jobs.
