From a discrepancy to a next step

Two exports. A reviewable report.

A missing event and a duplicated event need different responses. Start by locating the difference, then inspect the evidence before deciding what to do.

01

Bring your product usage.

Use the events recorded by your product: event ID, customer ID, timestamp and quantity. Include the metric when available.

02

Add the metered usage.

Upload the usage recorded by your meter or billing workflow. Common column names are inferred; review the mapping shown in the report.

03

Review what differs.

Inspect each finding, its supporting evidence, its status and the suggested next action. Check prices and the period before interpreting the financial impact.

This first check compares source events with metered usage. Checking invoice amounts, credits or effective prices requires additional files and compatible fields.

What to look for

More than missing usage.

The event-level scan also checks for duplicates, wrong quantities, customer or metric mismatches, late events, orphan metered rows and idempotency collisions.

What the result means

Evidence first.
Billing decisions second.

Findings are labelled confirmed, suspected or unverifiable. Those labels describe the reconciliation evidence—not a guarantee that money is owed or can be recovered.

MeterTruth does not issue invoices, apply credits or refunds, or automatically repair your billing data.

Prepare your first scan

Use test data before customer data.

Start with a small pair of source and metered usage exports for the same period. CSV, JSON, JSONL and NDJSON are supported, up to 5 MiB per file.

Before you rely on a result

A few important limits.

Do I need to connect Stripe?

No. The file-based scan compares two uploads without a billing-account connection. The separate Stripe connector needs access to Stripe and compares aggregate meter summaries; it does not prove every individual event was billed.

Does a finding mean revenue has been recovered?

No. A finding identifies a discrepancy. Financial exposure depends on the pricing inputs, period and available evidence. Billing changes and proof that a recovery action was applied remain outside this beta.

Where do my data and reports go?

The app processes original uploads in memory and does not retain the original files or API keys. It stores sanitized filenames, findings, evidence and summaries in SQLite on the machine running the app. On a hosted instance, that is the server—not your browser. Evidence can contain sensitive information. Vercel history is ephemeral and may disappear on restart.

Can it check invoice lines and credits too?

Yes, through optional compatible uploads. Invoice, credit and effective-price checks are separate from source-to-meter leakage to avoid double counting. Taxes, refunds and proof of completed recovery actions are not inferred. Review the documented input requirements before interpreting those checks.
